Tele-MANAS

telemanas.mohfw.gov.in
FreeGovernmentPhone

India's national tele-mental-health programme. Trained counsellors offer support for stress, anxiety, low mood and emotional crises, and can refer you onward to a specialist if needed.

  • How to reach: call 14416 or 1800-891-4416
  • Availability: 24×7, toll-free
  • Languages: many Indian languages
  • Best for: anyone who wants a free, confidential first conversation

iCall

icallhelpline.org
PhoneEmailChat

A psychosocial helpline run by the Tata Institute of Social Sciences. Trained mental health professionals offer counselling on relationships, stress, loneliness and other life difficulties.

  • How to reach: phone, email and chat (see site for current hours and number)
  • Cost: the helpline is free; check the site for any paid options
  • Languages: English, Hindi and several regional languages
  • Best for: talking things through with a qualified counsellor

Vandrevala Foundation

vandrevalafoundation.com
Free24×7Call / WhatsApp

A nonprofit offering round-the-clock counselling for people in emotional distress, including those having thoughts of self-harm. Counsellors listen without judgment.

  • How to reach: call 9999 666 555 or use the options on the website
  • Availability: any time of day or night
  • Languages: English, Hindi and some regional languages
  • Best for: talking at odd hours when you can't wait

YourDOST

yourdost.com
Online chatVideoPaid & free options

An online counselling platform that connects you with psychologists, coaches and experts. Popular with students and young professionals dealing with exam stress, career worries and relationships.

  • How to reach: sign up on the website and pick an expert
  • Cost: some sessions are paid; pricing is shown on the site
  • Languages: English and Hindi, others vary
  • Best for: students and working professionals

7 Cups

7cups.com
Free chatPeer listenersAnonymous

An online emotional-support community where trained volunteer listeners chat with you for free. It is peer support, not therapy, which makes it a gentle place to start when you just want to be heard.

  • How to reach: create a free account and start a chat
  • Cost: free listener chat; paid therapy is also offered
  • Languages: mostly English
  • Note: not a crisis service. If you're in danger, call 112 instead

What to expect when you reach out

  • You don't need the right words. "I'm not feeling okay" is enough to begin.
  • Most services are confidential, but ask how your information is handled.
  • You can end the conversation whenever you want.
  • If it doesn't feel like a fit, try a different person or service.
  • Wait times can vary. Helplines may take a few tries to connect.
  • Asking for help is a strong thing to do.
In danger or thinking about ending your life? Please call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 right now.
