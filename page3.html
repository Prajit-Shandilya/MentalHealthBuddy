Words to hold onto right now

Read slowly. Read them twice if you need to.

This feeling is real, but it is not permanent.
You don't have to solve everything tonight. Just get through the next few minutes.
Reaching out is not weakness. It's one of the bravest things a person can do.
You matter, and the people who love you would want to know you're hurting.
Pain can make the future look empty. It isn't. It just can't be seen from here.
If you're in danger, move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself and call someone.
breathe

Take one minute to ground yourself

  1. Breathe in slowly as the circle grows (4 seconds).
  2. Breathe out slowly as it shrinks (4 seconds).
  3. Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear.
  4. Sip some water. Then call or message someone you trust.
